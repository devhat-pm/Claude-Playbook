# Acme Employee Handbook (fictional, for cache tests)

## Working hours

Core hours are 10:00 to 15:00 in your local time zone. Outside core hours you may arrange your day as you like, provided you attend scheduled meetings and respond to pages when on call. Managers must not schedule recurring meetings outside core hours without the agreement of everyone invited.

## Remote work

All roles are remote-first. You may work from any country where we have a legal entity for up to 90 days per calendar year; longer stays need written approval from People Operations because of tax and immigration rules. Co-working memberships up to $250 per month are reimbursed.

## Equipment

New starters receive a laptop of their choice from the approved list, a 27-inch monitor, a keyboard, a mouse and a headset. Equipment is replaced every three years or when it fails. Lost or stolen equipment must be reported to IT within 24 hours so devices can be wiped remotely.

## Expenses

Submit expenses within 30 days through the finance portal with an itemised receipt. Meals while travelling are reimbursed up to $75 per day. Alcohol is not reimbursed except at approved team events. Expenses above $500 need pre-approval from your manager.

## Travel

Book flights and hotels through the travel desk. Economy class is standard for flights under six hours; premium economy is allowed for longer flights. Hotels should be within the city rate cap published on the travel page. Rail is preferred over flying for journeys under four hours.

## Paid time off

Everyone receives 25 days of paid vacation per year plus local public holidays. Up to five unused days carry over into the next year and expire on 31 March. Request time off at least two weeks in advance for absences longer than three days.

## Sick leave

Sick leave is unlimited for short illnesses. Tell your manager as early as possible on the first day. Absences longer than five consecutive working days need a doctor's note. Long-term illness is handled with People Operations under the disability and leave policy.

## Parental leave

Primary caregivers receive 20 weeks of fully paid leave and secondary caregivers receive 10 weeks, available within the first year after birth or adoption. A phased return of up to four weeks at 60 percent hours and full pay is offered after leave ends.

## Learning budget

Each employee has a $1,500 annual learning budget for courses, books, conferences and certifications. Unused budget does not roll over. Conference travel counts against the travel policy, not the learning budget.

## On-call

Engineers on the on-call rota receive a weekly stipend of $300 and a day off in lieu for every weekend day paged for more than two hours. Acknowledge pages within 15 minutes. Hand over open incidents in writing at the end of each shift.

## Security

Use the company password manager for every work account and enable hardware-key two-factor authentication. Never share credentials in chat or email. Report suspected phishing with the Report button in your mail client; do not forward suspicious messages to colleagues.

## Performance reviews

Reviews run twice a year, in April and October. They combine a self-review, peer feedback from three colleagues and a manager assessment. Compensation changes take effect on 1 July following the April cycle.

## Onboarding

Your first week follows a fixed plan. On day one you meet your manager and your onboarding buddy, receive your equipment and set up your accounts. On days two and three you complete the security, privacy and harassment-prevention courses, which are mandatory and must be finished before you get production access. By the end of week one you should have shipped one small change or completed one small task with your buddy's help. Your manager holds a check-in at 30, 60 and 90 days to review progress against the goals agreed on your first day. If anything in your setup is missing or broken, raise it in the onboarding channel rather than waiting.

## Probation

New employees serve a probation period of three months, extendable once by up to three more months if both sides agree that more time is needed. During probation the notice period is one week on either side. Your manager must give written feedback at the midpoint of probation. Probation ends automatically at the end of the period unless your manager has told you in writing that it is being extended or that your employment will not continue.

## Notice periods and leaving

After probation, the notice period is one month for individual contributors and two months for managers and above, unless your local contract says otherwise. Return all equipment within ten working days of your last day using the prepaid shipping label IT provides. Your accounts are disabled at 18:00 local time on your last day. Unused vacation days are paid out where local law requires it and otherwise forfeited. You may request a reference letter from People Operations at any time after leaving.

## Code of conduct

We expect everyone to treat colleagues, customers and partners with respect. Harassment, discrimination and retaliation are not tolerated in any setting, including chat, video calls, offsites and social events. Raise concerns with your manager, any member of People Operations, or the anonymous reporting line. Reports are investigated promptly and confidentially. Raising a concern in good faith will never count against you, even if the investigation does not substantiate it.

## Conflicts of interest

Disclose any outside employment, board seat, investment above $10,000 in a competitor, supplier or customer, or close personal relationship with someone you manage or buy from. Disclosures go to the legal team through the conflicts form and are reviewed within ten working days. Most disclosures are approved with simple safeguards, such as removing you from a purchasing decision. Failing to disclose is treated more seriously than the conflict itself.

## Gifts and hospitality

You may accept gifts and hospitality from business contacts worth up to $100 per item and $250 per contact per year. Cash, gift cards and anything offered during an active purchasing decision must always be declined. Record every gift above $50 in the gifts register within a week of receiving it. Giving gifts to government officials requires prior approval from the legal team regardless of value.

## Data protection

Customer data may only be accessed for a specific, documented support or engineering purpose. Production data must never be copied to a laptop, personal device or third-party tool that is not on the approved vendor list. Use the anonymised staging dataset for testing. Report any suspected data breach to the security team immediately, even if you are unsure; we have 72 hours to notify regulators in many jurisdictions and the clock starts when we become aware.

## Acceptable use of AI tools

You may use the AI assistants on the approved tools list for work. Do not paste customer personal data, credentials or unreleased financial results into any tool that is not on the list. You remain responsible for reviewing everything an AI tool produces before it is shipped, sent or published. Generated code goes through the same review process as any other code.

## Open source contributions

Contributions to open source projects during work time are encouraged when they relate to tools we use. Personal projects on your own time and equipment belong to you, provided they do not use confidential information or compete with our products. Before publishing a new open source project under the company name, get approval from the open source review group, which checks licensing and security.

## Communication norms

Default to public channels over direct messages so that decisions are searchable. Use threads to keep channels readable. Messages outside your recipient's working hours are fine, but nobody is expected to reply until their next working day unless they are on call. Decisions that affect more than one team should be written down in a short decision record and linked from the relevant project page.

## Meetings

Every meeting needs an agenda shared in advance and an owner who sends notes with decisions and action items afterwards. Default meeting length is 25 or 50 minutes to leave a buffer. Wednesday is a no-meeting day for everyone except customer-facing teams. If you are invited to a meeting without an agenda you may decline it politely and ask for one.

## Benefits

Health insurance is provided through the local benefits provider in each country, covering employees and dependants at no cost to the employee. A wellbeing allowance of $60 per month may be used for gym memberships, fitness classes, therapy apps or mental health services. The employee assistance programme offers six free confidential counselling sessions per issue per year.

## Retirement plans

Where a local retirement or pension plan exists, the company matches employee contributions up to 5 percent of base salary. Matching contributions vest immediately. You can change your contribution rate at any time through the benefits portal, and changes take effect from the next payroll.

## Payroll

Salaries are paid monthly on the last working day of the month in local currency. Payslips are available in the payroll portal from three days before payday. Report any payroll error to payroll support within five working days of payday so it can be corrected in the next cycle.

## Promotions

Promotions are considered during the October review cycle. Your manager writes a promotion case that shows sustained performance at the next level for at least six months, supported by examples and peer feedback. A calibration committee reviews all cases across the department to keep standards consistent. Outcomes are communicated by the end of November and take effect on 1 January.

## Internal transfers

After twelve months in your current role you may apply for open internal roles. Tell your current manager before your first interview. Internal candidates are interviewed on the same criteria as external ones, and the hiring manager must give written feedback to every internal candidate who is not selected.

## Referrals

If you refer a candidate who is hired and stays for at least six months, you receive a referral bonus of $3,000, or $5,000 for roles marked as hard to fill. Referrals must be submitted through the hiring portal before the candidate applies on their own. Hiring managers and interviewers on the same role are not eligible for the bonus.

## Offsites and team events

Each team may hold one in-person offsite per year within the per-head budget published by finance. Company-wide gatherings take place once a year. Attendance at offsites is encouraged but never required, and alternatives are provided for anyone who cannot travel. Team social events are reimbursed up to $40 per person per quarter.

## Volunteering

Everyone receives two paid volunteering days per year to support a registered charity or community organisation of their choice. Book volunteering days like vacation and select the volunteering category. The company also matches personal donations to registered charities up to $500 per employee per year.

## Policy changes

This handbook is reviewed every six months by People Operations and the legal team. Material changes are announced in the company channel at least two weeks before they take effect, with a summary of what changed and why. Where this handbook conflicts with your local employment contract or local law, the contract or law takes precedence.
